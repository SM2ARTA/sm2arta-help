Transactions


Overview


Transactions are the operational record layer of Asset Tracking in SM2ARTA. Every time an asset is received, dispatched, delivered, transferred, returned, adjusted, or dissolved, that activity is captured as a transaction.


Together with Nomenclatures (which define the physical assets) and Reports (which provide visibility and audit evidence), Transactions form the core of the Asset Tracking functionality.


SM2ARTA enforces a single, non-negotiable principle: no physical movement, adjustment, or exception is valid unless it is recorded as a system transaction. This ensures that the system always reflects the real-world location, quantity, and condition of every asset, and provides a single source of truth across warehouses and venues.


Purpose of Transactions


Transactions ensure that every asset movement across the event footprint is:


  • Recorded under the correct transaction type;
  • Supported by documents and photographic evidence;
  • Verified and approved by the responsible Material Planning Manager;
  • Traceable from procurement through final dissolution.


In doing so, transactions make visible the 5 Ws of asset tracking: What was moved, Where it went, When it happened, Who handled it, and Why it moved.


What a Transaction Records


Each transaction captures a consistent set of information:


  • Source (From) and destination locations (To) — the origin and target (Warehouse or Venue);
  • Nomenclature code and name — the asset being moved;
  • Quantity and form factor — how much, and its state;
  • Transaction Type — Inbound, Moving, Outbound, Damage Report or Dissolution;
  • Supporting documents — such as ASN, OOR, TM or any other attached documents;
  • Photos — for verification and audit.


The Nomenclature itself never changes — only its location, quantity, status, and condition change, and only through a transaction. Manual or offline adjustments are not permitted.


Transaction Types


Every transaction belongs to one of five transaction types, which describe the nature of the asset movement or change:


  • Inbound — assets entering a supply chain at any node (e.g. receiving at a warehouse or venue);
  • Moving - assets moved inside the supply chain between nodes (e.g. from warehouse to venue); 
  • Outbound — assets leaving a supply chain for external location (e.g. returning to external supplier).
  • Damage report - reclassification of nomenclature to damaged category;
  • Dissolution - reclassification of nomenclature to any of the dissolution category created in the system.


Stock Correction is an adjustment to recorded quantities, performed via inbound/outbound transaction.


Transaction Status


Each transaction carries a status that reflects its stage in the workflow, allowing users to distinguish in-progress movements from completed and confirmed records. Transaction statuses are described in the Transactions Statuses section.


Roles and Permissions


Access to transactions functionality depends on the user's role and assigned permissions:


Default settings:


Role

Transactions Functionality

Material Planning Manager
  • Can view and create all transactions across every warehouse and venue; 
  • Can update status and complete every transaction in the system.

Venue Logistics Manager
  • Can view and create transactions only for the Venues and Warehouse(s) they have been granted access to;
  • Can not update status of transaction;
  • Can complete only own transactions or transactions they have been given access to.

Functional Material Planner
  • Does not have access to Asset Tracking module.


Core Principle


If an asset movement or adjustment is not recorded as a transaction in SM2ARTA, it is not valid. This principle guarantees that all assets moving through the Event Logistics supply chain remain fully visible, controlled, and accountable.
